Experimental Design

Research Design & Variable Operationalization

Quantitative comparative framework, clinical variable definitions, and experimental validity controls

SLIDE 05 / 07
Independent Variables (X)

Physiological Predictors

Demographics: Age (29–77 yrs), Biological Sex.
Symptom Class: Chest pain type (Typical, Atypical, Non-anginal, Asymptomatic).
Hemodynamics: Resting BP, Max Heart Rate, Resting Electrocardiogram.
Ischemia Markers: ST depression (oldpeak), Peak ST slope.
Fluoroscopy: Major vessels (0–3), Thallium scintigraphy.
Dependent Variable (Y)

Clinical Target

Diagnostic Standard: Coronary artery stenosis ≥ 50%.
Binary Encoding: 0 = Healthy (54.1%), 1 = CAD Present (45.9%).
Cohort Split: 164 negative controls, 139 positive cases.
Clinical Utility: Non-invasive early triage risk stratification.
Target Goal: Minimize diagnostic latency in emergency triage.
Experimental Controls

Methodological Rigor

Leakage Shield: Transformers fit strictly inside training folds.
Stratified Balancing: Identical class proportions across all folds.
Baseline Controls: Benchmarked against standard L1/L2 Logistic.
Calibration: Reliability curves & Brier score optimization.
Statistical Tests: 95% bootstrap confidence interval bounds.
